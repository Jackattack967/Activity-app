Activity Schedule Dashboard shows public drop-in recreation schedules from {{ source_names|join(' and ') }}, and can alert you when a spot opens in an activity you've chosen to watch. This page explains exactly what it stores, who else sees it, where it is kept, and how to remove it.

It is an unofficial, personal project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by any municipality.

Using it without an account

You can browse every schedule without signing in. No account is created, no cookies are set, and your filter choices are saved only in your own browser's local storage — clearing your browser data removes them.

Two things still happen, as they do on any website. The server that hosts this site keeps standard request logs — your IP address, the time, the page requested, and your browser's user-agent string — which are used to keep the site running and to investigate faults and abuse, not to build a profile of you. And your internet provider and the hosting provider necessarily carry the traffic. There is no analytics service and there are no advertising trackers on this site.

What is stored if you sign in

Signing in is optional, and only needed to watch activities and receive alerts.

  • From your Google account: your email address, name, profile picture URL, and the account identifier Google provides. No password is ever received or stored — Google performs the sign-in and only confirms who you are.
  • Your preferences: the activity and location filters you set as defaults, and whether you opted in to email alerts.
  • Activities you watch: the name and venue of each starred activity, and for one-off watches, the date.
  • Notification details: if you enable alerts, the push subscription your browser generates for each device. This identifies the browser to a push service, not you personally.
  • The date your account was created.

What is never collected

  • Passwords, for this app or for any city's booking system
  • Payment or financial information — all registration and payment happens on the city's own site
  • Location data, contacts, photos, or anything else from your device
  • Analytics or advertising trackers — there are none

How it is used

Only to run the features you asked for: to show your saved filters, to know which activities to watch, and to send you an alert when one opens. Your information is never sold, rented, traded, or used for advertising, and it is never disclosed to anyone for their own purposes.

Who else handles your information

Running this app does mean other companies handle your information on its behalf. They are permitted to use it only to perform the job described here, and not for anything of their own. This is the complete list:

  • Render — hosts the app. All traffic to this site passes through Render's servers, and its request logs are described above.
  • Neon — provides the database that holds everything listed under "What is stored if you sign in".
  • Google — performs the sign-in, so Google knows you have signed in to this app. If you are signed in, your profile picture is loaded directly from Google's servers when a page opens, so your browser contacts Google at that moment.
  • Your browser's push service — for browser notifications: Google for Chrome and Edge, Mozilla for Firefox, Apple for Safari. The service delivers the notification to your device, but the contents are encrypted so it cannot read them. It can see that a message was sent to your device and when.

Where your information is stored

Your information is stored and processed outside Canada. Render and Neon are United States companies and hold the app and its database on servers in the United States.{% if email_provider_name == "Resend" %} Resend is also a United States company.{% elif email_provider_name == "Brevo" %} Brevo is a French company and processes alert emails in the European Union.{% endif %} Google operates globally.

While information is in another country it is subject to that country's laws, and courts, law enforcement and national security authorities there may be able to compel access to it without notice to you. Using this app with an account means accepting that. If you would rather not, you can use the dashboard without signing in — nothing about you is stored then.

How long it is kept

  • Your account and everything attached to it is kept until you delete it. Deleting is immediate and permanent (see below).
  • One-off watches for a single date are deleted automatically once that date has passed.
  • Push subscriptions are deleted automatically when your browser reports that they have expired.
  • Server request logs are kept for as long as the hosting provider retains them, which is a short rolling window, and are then discarded.

Cookies

One cookie is used, and only after you sign in: a session cookie that keeps you signed in. It contains no advertising or tracking identifier. Browsing without signing in sets no cookies at all. There are no third-party cookies.

Your choices and your rights

  • Don't sign in. Every schedule is visible without an account, and nothing about you is stored.
  • Turn alerts off at any time under Menu → Preferences, separately for email and for browser notifications. Turning email alerts off stops all alert email immediately.
  • See what is held about you, and have it corrected. Under Canadian and British Columbia privacy law you may ask for a copy of the personal information held about you and ask for anything inaccurate to be fixed. Your name, email and picture come from your Google account — correcting them in Google and signing in again is usually the fastest fix.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account, which removes everything at once.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account and everything listed above at any time, and it is removed immediately and permanently rather than deactivated.

  • In the app: Menu → Preferences → Delete my account
  • Or from this page: Delete your account

Deleting removes your profile, preferences, watched activities and notification subscriptions. You can sign in again afterwards, which creates a fresh empty account.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your device and the server is encrypted. Sign-in is handled by Google, so this app never sees a password. Access to the database is limited to the app itself. No online service can promise perfect security, but nothing is stored that is not needed for a feature you asked for.

Children

Signing in requires a Google account, which Google does not issue to children under 13. Accounts on this app are therefore intended for people aged 13 and over. Anyone of any age can browse the schedules without signing in, since that stores nothing. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, use the deletion page above, or get in touch and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If what is collected or who handles it changes, this page is updated and the "last updated" date at the top changes with it. Material changes affecting signed-in users will also be shown in the app.
