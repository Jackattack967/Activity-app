What gets deleted

Everything held about you, permanently and straight away:

  • Your profile — email, name and profile picture from Google sign-in
  • Your saved filter preferences
  • Every activity you're watching
  • Notification subscriptions on all your devices

This cannot be undone. You can sign in again later, which creates a new empty account. Nothing is kept in the meantime.

Registrations you made on a city's own booking system are held by that city, not by this app, and are unaffected. Contact the city directly for those.
